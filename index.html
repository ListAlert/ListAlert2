How it works

Never forget a key item again!

1

Build & maintain your list & cookbook

Manually add items and recurring staples, extract full recipes from your camera roll, or let ListAlert curate meals just for you. Tag items to favorite stores/delivery apps, and plan your trip.

2

Build your pantry

Track items and staples you have on hand, and let ListAlert automatically exclude them from planned trips.

3

Plan your week

Using intervals of 3-5-7 days, plan your meals with saved recipes in your cookbook, and augment with your saved list. Open Trip Planning Mode and let anyone in your household add/remove as needed.

4

Shop your way

Get reminders when you're nearby favorite stores with tagged items, and shop with your favorite stores/delivery apps without duplicating lists.

5

Track your history

Get better visibility into your purchasing habits to help plan & save.
