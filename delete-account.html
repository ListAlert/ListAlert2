Delete Your ListAlert Account

ListAlert  ·  Last updated: September 28, 2026

You can delete your ListAlert account and its data at any time. Use the in-app option if you still have the app installed. Otherwise, send us a request by email.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open ListAlert and go to Stores & Household → Household.
  2. Tap "Delete my account and data" and confirm.
  3. Uninstall the app to remove any data stored only on your device.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Email info@listalert.app

Use the subject line "Delete my ListAlert data" and include either:

  • the email address you added to your account, or
  • your household name and your display name, so we can find your records.

We will reply to confirm once deletion is complete.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record, including any email address you added
  • Your household membership and display name
  • Any email sign-up you made through the app
  • If you are the last member of your household: the household itself and all of its data — list items, saved recipes and recipe photos, meal plans, trips, stores, pantry, and history

What may be kept

  • Shared household content. If other people are still in your household, the shared list and recipes stay available to them, because they belong to the household rather than to you alone.
  • Backups. Deleted data may stay in routine encrypted database backups for up to 30 days, after which it is permanently removed.

Data that lives only on your phone (for example, store alert preferences and Kroger connection tokens) is removed when you uninstall the app.

For full details on what ListAlert collects and how it is used, see our Privacy Policy.
