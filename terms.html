1. Description of Service

ListAlert is a personal grocery list, recipe catalog, and household coordination application. Core features include:

  • My List — a personal grocery list with categories, recurring items, and store tags.
  • My Cookbook — a personal recipe catalog that extracts ingredients from photos, URLs, and text descriptions using AI assistance.
  • Stores & Household — saved store locations with optional GPS proximity alerts, and household sharing for joint lists.
  • Retailer handoff — the ability to send a curated ingredient list to a supported delivery provider (currently Walmart and Kroger; Instacart IDP when available) so you can complete a purchase on that provider's platform.
Neutrality principle. ListAlert does not pre-select products, include affiliate links, or favor any particular retailer or brand. We capture what you want at the category level ("dish soap," "cumin") and leave product selection to you and the retailer.

4.1 Kroger / Kroger-Family Stores

ListAlert offers an optional integration with the Kroger Products and Cart APIs. To use this feature:

  • You will be redirected to Kroger's OAuth authorization page, where you authenticate directly with Kroger using your own Kroger account credentials. ListAlert never receives or stores your Kroger username or password.
  • After authorization, Kroger issues ListAlert a time-limited access token. This token is stored encrypted on your device and is used exclusively to add your selected ingredients to your Kroger cart.
  • The token exchange is handled through a secure server-side proxy that holds the Kroger client secret; that secret is never transmitted to your device.
  • You may revoke ListAlert's access to your Kroger account at any time through your Kroger account settings at kroger.com.
  • ListAlert is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Kroger Co.

Your use of Kroger's services is subject to Kroger's Terms and Conditions and Kroger's Privacy Policy. Use of the Kroger API is subject to the Kroger Developer Terms of Use.

4.4 AI-Powered Recipe Extraction

Recipe extraction uses Claude, an AI model operated by Anthropic, PBC. When you submit a photo, URL, or text for recipe extraction, that content is transmitted to our secure server-side proxy and then to Anthropic for processing. We do not share your name, account identity, or personal details with Anthropic. See Section 6 and our Privacy Policy for more detail.

ListAlert also offers optional AI image generation: if you generate an illustrative picture for a recipe, the recipe name and prompt are sent through our proxy to Cloudflare's Workers AI. No account identity or personal details are sent.
